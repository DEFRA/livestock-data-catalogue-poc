Privacy

This prototype is run by the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Defra), which is the data controller.

It does not ask for personal data, and it does not save what you search for. The people, email addresses and data assets you see in it are made up.

Your search words are part of the web address of the results page. The platform the prototype runs on may keep logs of the web addresses it is asked for, with the IP address of the device that asked, to keep the service running and secure. Do not type personal information into the search box.

If someone enters a wrong password, the prototype remembers the network address it came from for 1 minute, to stop passwords being guessed. It does not save the address.

If you create or change data assets, your changes are kept in the prototype's memory, not saved to its data, and only your browser can see them. They are deleted 4 hours after your last change, when you sign out, when you reset the prototype in Prototype settings, or when the prototype restarts. Do not type personal information into the forms. You choose people from a list of made-up people.

It only uses essential cookies. Read more about the cookies we use.

If you take part in a research session, the research team will tell you how they use and keep what you say.

Read Defra's personal information charter to find out how Defra uses personal data, how to contact its data protection officer, and your rights, including how to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
